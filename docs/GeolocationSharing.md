# Location Sharing – User Guide

This guide explains how location sharing works in NeuroEase for **patients** and **caregivers**.

---

## For Patients

### What is location sharing?

Location sharing lets your caregiver see where you are on a map. This helps them check that you are safe and know when you might have left a familiar area (like home).

### How do I turn it on?

1. Open the NeuroEase app and go to **Profile**.
2. Find the **Location sharing** section.
3. Turn the toggle **On**.

When you turn it on, your phone will ask for permission. Choose **Allow** or **Always** so the app can use your location.

### What happens when it’s on?

- Your location is sent to your caregiver’s dashboard.
- The map updates automatically so your caregiver sees where you are.
- If you leave a safe zone (for example, home), your caregiver gets an alert.

### Can I turn it off?

Yes. Go to **Profile** → **Location sharing** and turn the toggle **Off**. Your caregiver will no longer receive your location.

### When is my location sent?

- **If you use the app in a browser or “Add to Home Screen”:** Your location is sent while the app is open.
- **If you use the NeuroEase app from the App Store or Play Store:** Your location can be sent even when the app is closed or your screen is locked (24/7 tracking).

### What happens when I lose internet?

If you have no internet connection (e.g. in a tunnel or poor signal), the app stores your location updates locally. When you are back online, it sends them automatically. Your caregiver will still see your movements once the connection is restored.

### What if I see a warning about location permission?

If you see a message like “Location permission is off,” your phone has blocked the app from using your location. Go to your phone’s **Settings** → **Privacy** → **Location Services** and allow NeuroEase to use your location.

## For Caregivers

### How do I see a patient’s location?

1. Log in to the caregiver dashboard.
2. Go to the **Location** page.
3. Choose the patient from the dropdown.
4. The map shows their latest location and any safe zones you have set.

The map refreshes automatically every 30 seconds while you have the page open.

### What are safe zones?

Safe zones are areas you define (for example, home or a day centre). When the patient is inside a zone, their marker is green. When they are outside, it turns red and you receive an alert.

### How do I add a safe zone?

1. On the Location page, select the patient.
2. Click **Add safe zone**.
3. Click on the map where the centre should be, or search for a place.
4. Give it a name (e.g. “Home”) and choose the size.
5. Click **Save**.

### What alerts do I get?

- **Left safe zone** – The patient has moved outside a safe zone.
- **Returned to safe zone** – The patient has moved back inside.

You can see these in the **Location alerts** section and filter by Today, This week, or This month.

### How do I get directions to the patient?

Click the patient’s marker on the map, then click **Go to location**. This opens Google Maps so you can start turn-by-turn navigation.

## Privacy and Data

### What data is stored?

When location sharing is on, we store:

- Latitude and longitude
- Time of the update
- Accuracy of the location (when available)

### Who can see my location?

Only your assigned caregiver(s) can see your location. It is not shared with anyone else.

### Who can turn location sharing on or off?

Only the patient can turn location sharing on or off in their app. Caregivers cannot change this setting.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|--------|-------------|
| I don’t see the patient on the map | Check that the patient has turned on location sharing in their app. |
| Location hasn’t updated for a while | If the patient uses the app in a browser, location is only sent while the app is open. Ask them to open the app. If they were offline, queued updates are sent when back online. |
| I get “Location permission is off” | The patient needs to allow location in their phone’s Settings. |
| I want 24/7 tracking | The patient needs to use the NeuroEase app from the App Store or Play Store (not the browser). |
